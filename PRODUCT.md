# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Explorers, plant conservationists, fantasy garden gamers, field rangers, and system administrators. They use Plant Guardian to track flora sightings, play conservation minigames, earn Nature Coins (NC) and EXP, and manage plant databases in field journal / herbarium aesthetics.

## Product Purpose
Plant Guardian merges gamified plant conservation with a field journal interface. It enables users to document flora, explore interactive maps, play fantasy garden minigames, and participate in community plant protection.

## Brand Personality
- **Tactile Botanical**: Earthy, parchment-rich, warm field journal aesthetic with deep forest greens (#1F3D20), gold (#FFD700), and crisp organic tones.
- **Engaging & Gamified**: Clear progression badges (Level, EXP, NC coins), whimsical yet structured UI.
- **Authentic & Legible**: High-contrast typography (Baloo 2 headings, Nunito body, Fraunces serif headlines, IBM Plex Mono code badges), zero unreadable gray-on-gray text.

## Anti-references
- Generic corporate SaaS dashboards with floating cards and soft 32px drop shadows.
- Sterile white-and-gray AI slop interfaces without personality or domain context.
- Decorative side-stripe borders on callout boxes or flat uninspired button grids.
- Hover scale animations on non-interactive images.

## Design Principles
1. **Botanical Tactility**: Every element should feel like a physical specimen card, field journal entry, or wooden ranger plaque.
2. **High-Contrast Clarity**: Text must remain crisp and readable (>= 4.5:1 contrast ratio) against warm parchment or deep green backgrounds.
3. **Intentional Affordances**: Interactive elements (buttons, pills, tabs) must have clear state feedback (hover, focus, active scale) while non-actionable media remains still.
4. **Cohesive Color Commitment**: Rely on the established brand ramp (Deep Forest `#1F3D20`, Parchment `#F5F4DA`, Warm Sand `#EDE6D3`, Gold `#FFD700`, Terracotta/Ranger `#8B6A4C`).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast compliance across all text labels, badges, and interactive controls.
- Full support for reduced motion preference.
- Clear semantic HTML hierarchy and visible focus rings on keyboard navigation.
